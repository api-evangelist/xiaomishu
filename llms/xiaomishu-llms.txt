# Xiaomishu (订餐小秘书)

> Xiaomishu ("Dining Little Secretary") is a Shanghai restaurant reservation and dining-marketing technology company operating since 2005 at xiaomishu.com and the 021-57575777 booking hotline. Its platforms — 订餐小秘书 (Xiaomishu), 宴位 (Yanwei, AI-driven dynamic pricing for restaurant private rooms), and 高能店长 (Gaoneng Dianzhang) — let diners book tables and discounted private-room packages at Shanghai restaurants via web, WAP, phone, WeChat mini-program, and customer-service channels. The company publishes no public developer API, developer portal, or machine-readable API surface.

## Company

- [Website (订餐小秘书)](http://www.xiaomishu.com/): Shanghai restaurant search and online reservation site (HTTP only; the HTTPS certificate does not match the domain)
- [About / Contact (关于我们)](http://www.xiaomishu.com/about/aboutus/): company profile — 22 years in dining reservations, Yanwei dynamic-pricing products, strategic investment from Ctrip and SIG
- [Mobile site](https://m.xiaomishu.com/): WAP booking surface (also m.57.cn)
- [Food-safety management rules (餐饮安全管理办法)](http://www.xiaomishu.com/about/rule/)
